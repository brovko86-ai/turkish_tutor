Imperative cases are used in sentences that request or suggest an action to be taken in Turkish. The imperative mood refers to an instruction directed to others to perform an action. Imperative verbs can be used in two ways, positive and negative. This is especially common for the second person singular and plural (sen and siz). It can also be used for third person singular and plural (o, onlar).


POSITIVE 
They are verbs that do not have suffixes and are made up of a direct verb root. It is often used in daily life.


(verb root) 


(Sen) Gel! - Come!
(Sen) Yaz! - Write!
(Sen) Oku! - Read!
(Sen) Gülümse! - Smile!
(Sen) Dinle! - Listen!
(Sen) Sus! - Be quiet!


When we talk to more than one person, or in an formal situation:


(verb root) + (-ın, -in, -un, -ün)


(Siz) Gelin! - Come!
(Siz) Yazın! - Write!
(Siz) Okuyun! - Oku!
(Siz) Gülümseyin! - Smile!
(Siz) Dinleyin! - Listen!
(Siz) Susun! - Be quiet!


This construction is used when giving orders to a person or group.


(verb root) + (-sın, -sin, -sun, -sün) FOR PLURAL: + (-ler, -lar)


(O) Gelsin! - Let him come!              (Onlar) Gelsinler! - Let them come!                         
(O) Yazsın! - Let her write!               (Onlar) Yazsınler! - Let them write!
(O) Okusun! - Let him read!             (Onlar) Okusunlar! - Let them read!
(O) Gülümsesin! - Let her smile!       (Onlar) Gülümsesinler! let them 
(O) Dinlesin! - Let him listen!           (Onlar) Dinlesinler! - 
(O) Sussun! - Let her be quiet!         (Onlar) Sussunlar! - 
NEGATIVE
It is created with the suffix "-me/-ma". Indicates that we do not want an action to be taken.


Örnekler:


(verb root) + (-me, -ma) 


(Sen) Koşma! - Don't run!
(Sen) İzleme! - Don't watch!
(Sen) Konuşma! - Don't speak!
(Sen) Gitme! - Don’t go!
(Sen) Alma! - Don’t take!
(Sen) Yürüme! - Don’t walk!


When we talk to more than one person, or in an formal situation, we add the suffix "-in, -in, -un, -un" after “-me, -ma”.


(verb root) + (-me, -ma) + (-y) + (-ın, -in, -un, -ün)


(Siz) Koşmayın! - Don't run!
(Siz) İzlemeyin! - Don't watch!
(Siz) Konuşmayın! - Don't speak!
(Siz) Gitmeyin! - Don’t go!
(Siz) Almayın! - Don’t take!
(Siz) Yürümeyin! - Don’t walk!
