We use the "Definite Past Tense" to describe a situation or action that happened in the past. Definite past tense is similar to ordinary past tense in English. We use it when we know for sure that something has taken place. In other words when there is certainty about something that happened. The definite past tense attaches the suffixes "-dı, -di, -du, -dü" to the verb root. 


POSITIVE 


(verb root) + (-dı, -di, -du, -dü) + (possessive suffix)


For positive past tense sentences, after the suffix "-di, -di, -du, -du", we use possessive suffixes to shorten the pronunciation.Because in Turkish, only this suffix ends with a vowel. Therefore, the suffixes you need to add for personal pronouns are as follows:


ben
	sen
	o
	biz
	siz
	onlar
	-m
	-n
	NOTHING
	-k
	-nız, -niz, -nuz, -nüz
	-lar, -ler
	

DİKKAT: As you can see, the letter "-k" is the exception for the personal pronoun "biz-we". And for the personal pronoun "onlar-they", as usual "-lar, -ler” are used.


okumak
	to read
	izlemek
	to watch
	(Ben) okudum
	I read
	(Ben) izledim
	I watched
	(Sen) okudun
	You read
	(Sen) izledin
	You watched
	(O) okudu
	He/she read
	(O) izledi
	He/she watched
	(Biz) okuduk
	We read
	(Biz) izledik
	We watched
	(Siz) okudunuz
	You read
	(Siz) izlediniz
	You watched
	(Onlar) okudular
	They read
	(Onlar) izlediler
	They watched

QUESTION


(verb root) + (-dı, -di, -du, -dü) + (possessive suffix) + (mı mi mu mü)


Unlike the present continuous tense logic, the question suffix is ​​placed at the end of the sentence. 


(Actually, there is no exceptional rule as it seems.
Here we use "possessive suffixes”. Not “personal suffixes". 
And personal suffixes are always placed at the end of the sentence.)


ben
	sen
	o
	biz
	siz
	onlar
	-m
	-n
	NOTHING
	-k
	-nız,-niz, -nuz, -nüz
	-lar, -ler
	

bulmak
	to find
	seçmek
	to choose
	(Ben) buldum mu?
	Did I find?
	(Ben) seçtim mi?
	Did I choose?
	(Sen) buldun mu?
	Did you find?
	(Sen) seçtin mi?
	Did you choose?
	(O) buldu mu?
	Did he/she find?
	(O) seçti mi?
	Did he choose?
	(Biz) bulduk mu?
	Did we find?
	(Biz) seçtik mi?
	Did we choose?
	(Siz) buldunuz mu?
	Did you find?
	(Siz) seçtiniz mi?
	Did you choose?
	Onlar buldu mu?
	Did they find?
	Onlar seçti mi?
	Did they choose?
