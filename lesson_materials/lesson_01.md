3. Turkish is an agglutinative language.
Basically suffixes (endings) are added to the end of the word.


Rahat -comfort/comfortable- (adjective)
Rahatsız -uncomfortable- (adjective)
Rahatsızlık -discomfort/disease- (noun)
Rahatsızlıklar -diseases- (plural)

5. Turkish is a gender free language.


he = o
she = o
it = o


“He/she/it” is going - “O” gidiyor
“O gidiyor” in this sentence we can’t tell if it’s a man, a woman, an animal or maybe an object. 


6. Turkish doesn’t have many irregular forms.


For example, many verbs and some nouns in English do not follow the general grammatical rule. Also, the pronunciation of words is different from the way they are spelled. In this sense, there are very few exceptions in Turkish grammar, which makes it easier to learn the language. What we need to do is not to memorize the exception words, but to understand the logic of the grammar and that will be enough.


7. Turkish is not a phonetic language. Not every letter in the Turkish alphabet is always read as it is written. So, Turkish is very close to the phonetic language, but it is not.

8. Turkish has a different  sentence structure:


ENGLISH: Subject + verb + object
TURKISH: Subject + object + verb 


I eat an apple = Ben bir elma yerim.


But it's more flexible than other languages. The following sentences all mean the same thing, although in different order:


Ben elma yerim.
Ben yerim elma.
Elma yerim ben.
Elma ben yerim.
Yerim ben elma.
Yerim elma ben.


Because in Turkish, there are certain suffixes that each word follows. Therefore, even if the sentence order changes, the meaning does not change. We will understand this better in future lessons. However, this is the order we generally follow in daily life, TV series, movies and news.

10. The personal pronoun is always optional in the subject position. However, the personal suffix is ​​mandatory in any sentence without exception. You can not complete a sentence without adding it. Now let's explain it again with the example above:


Ben kitap okuyorum (CORRECT) = You can use both pronoun and personal suffix.


Kitap okuyorum (CORRECT)  = You can only use the personal suffix.


Ben kitap okuyor (MISTAKE) = If you remove the personal suffix from the sentence, the sentence will not be complete and will be incorrect.
