Before we start learning this suffix, let's take a look at the case suffixes in general. There are five case suffixes in Turkish. These are:


CASES →
	LOCATIVE
	DATIVE
	ABLATIVE
	INSTRUMENTAL
	ACCUSATIVE
	TÜRKÇE →
	-de, -da
	-e, -a
	-den, -dan
	-le, -la
	-ı, -i, -u, -ü
	MEANING →
	in, on,at 
	to (direction)
	from
	with
	like “the”
	

These are the most important suffixes in Turkish. Because every verb in Turkish has a suffix followed by it. But don't worry, you don't need to memorize it as most of it makes sense. You will only learn very few of these verbs by hearing or practicing. (If you are wondering about this subject now, you can take a look at the "LIST OF VERBS" document I shared with you)


We discussed the suffix "-de, -da" and "-e, -a" in our previous documents and made exercises. Today, we will continue with the suffix “-den -dan” it shows the state of "leaving". 


Now let's practice some of the most used verbs in daily life:


(-den, -dan gitmek:to go from)
Nereden Ankara’ya gidiyorsun? - From where you are going to Ankara?
İstanbul'dan Ankara'ya gidiyorum. - I am going to Ankara from Istanbul. 


Evden markete kaç dakika? - How many minutes from home to market?
Evden markete 15 dakika. - 15 minutes from home to market.


Otelden taksiye biniyoruz. - We take a taxi from the hotel.


(-e, -a binmek:to get in -any kind of transport vehicle)
(-den, -dan inmek:to get off -any kind of transport vehicle)


(-den, -dan dönmek:to go back)
Sinemadan ne zaman dönüyorlar? - When do they come back from the cinema?
Sinemadan akşam on birde dönüyorlar.- They return from the cinema at eleven in the evening.


İşten eve otuz dakikada dönüyor. - He gets home from work in thirty minutes.


Okuldan otobüsle dönüyor. - He returns from school by bus.


(-den, -dan gelmek:to come)
Almanya'dan Türkiye'ye hangi ay geliyorsun? - What month are you coming from Germany to Turkiye?
Almanya'dan Türkiye'ye bu Haziran geliyorum. - I am coming to Turkiye from Germany this June.


Her yaz İstanbul'dan İzmir'e tatil için geliyoruz. - Every summer we come to Izmir from Istanbul for a holiday.


Her gün şehir merkezinden geliyorsun. - You come from the city center every day.


(-den, -dan kalkmak:to get up)
Masadan kalkıyorum ve kahve hazırlıyorum. - I get up from the table and make coffee.


Yataktan kalkıyorum ve tuvalete gidiyorum. - I get out of bed and go to the bathroom.


(-den, -dan korkmak:to be afraid)
Neyden korkuyorlar? - What are they afraid of?
Köpekten korkuyorlar. - They are afraid of the dog.


Kimden korkuyorsun? - Who are you afraid of?
Ondan korkuyorum. - I am afraid of him.


(-den, -dan nefret etmek:to hate)
Soğandan nefret ediyoruz. - We hate onions.
Sigaradan nefret ediyorsun. - You hate cigarettes.
Kıştan nefret ediyorlar. - They hate winter.


(-den, -dan para çekmek:withdraw money)
Bankadan para çekiyorlar. - They withdraw money from the bank.
ATM'den para çekebilirsin. - You can withdraw money from the ATM.
Hesaptan para çekiyorum. - I withdraw money from the account.
