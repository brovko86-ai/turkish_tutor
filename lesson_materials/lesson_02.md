VOWEL HARMONY


Almost every suffix in Turkish has more than one form. We will know what form to take according to the last vowel of the word. There are two kinds of "Vowel Harmony". 


a. Major Vowel Harmony: For suffixes with 4 different variants.


if the last vowel ends in "a” or “ı”  →  choose the form with the "ı" one.
if the last vowel ends in "e” or “i”  →  choose the form with the "i" one.
if the last vowel ends in "o” or “u” →  choose the form with the "u" one.
if the last vowel ends in "ö” or “ü” →  choose the form with the "ü" one.


BEN - I: “-ım, -im, -um, -üm” 
SEN - YOU: “-sın, -sin, -sun, -sün” 
O - HE,SHE, IT: “No personal suffix!” 
BİZ - WE: “-ız, -iz, -uz, -üz” 
SİZ - YOU: “-sınız, -siniz, -sunuz, -sünüz” 
ONLAR - THEY: “-lar, -ler” 


Now that we have added the personal suffixes ↑ 
Let's practice and understand the logic!


(uzun:tall) - I am tall: (Ben) uzunum


(kısa:short) - You are short: (Sen) kısasın


(zengin:rich) - She is rich: O zengin.


(fakir:poor) - We are poor: (Biz) fakiriz


(iyi:good) - You (in plural) are good: (Siz) iyisiniz


(kötü:bad) - They are bad: (Onlar) kötü














b. Minor Vowel Harmony: For suffixes with 2 different variants.


if the last vowel ends in "a, ı, o, u” → choose the form with the "a" one.
if the last vowel ends in "e, i, ö, ü”  →choose the form with the "e" one.


(-e, -a: “to”) - to home: eve
(-de, -da: “in, on, at”) - at restaurant: restoranda
(-den, -dan: “from”) - from the window: pencereden
(-le, -la: “with”) - with Ahmet: Ahmetle
(-ler, -lar: “plural”) - Cups: bardaklar

NOT: If a suffix that starts with a vowel is added to a word that ends with a vowel, we must put one of the separator letter between it. In this way, we make the pronunciation softer and easier.


There are three separator letters in total. These are "-y", "-n" and "-s". And we use the letter "-y" with personal suffixes. Mesela, 


BEN - I: “-ım, -im, -um, -üm” 


güçlü+üm = güçlüüm = MISTAKE
güçlü+y+üm = güçlüyüm = CORRECT


tatlı+ım = tatlıım = MISTAKE
tatlı+y+ım = tatlıyım = CORRECT


kısa+ım = kısaım = MISTAKE
kısa+y+ım = kısayım = CORRECT

NOT: We have three different options in "-ler, -lar". 
Use the suffix "onlar" or "-ler, -lar" or “both”. Mesela,


MOST USED: Onlar doktor = They are doctors
SOMETIMES USED: Doktorlar = They are doctors
LESS USED: Onlar doktorlar = They are doctors


It's all grammatically correct, you can use any you want. We usually don't use the last one much because there is no need to repeat the plural twice.

	NOT: There is an exception to the "-lar, -ler" suffix with the question suffix. In no case do the question suffix and the plural suffix come to the same word. And as it should always be, the person suffix "-ler, -lar" does not come to the end of the sentence in that case.


General rule:Güzel miler? (MISTAKE)
Exceptional rule:Güzeller mi? (CORRECT)


-ler, -lar: they/plural suffix


But actually our exception rule goes like this: Güzeller mi? (CORRECT)


NOT: “Personal suffixes” are always at the end of the sentence. (except -ler, -lar which is explained above)


NOT: "değil-not" is always used with nouns or adjectives. It never comes to make a verb negative.
