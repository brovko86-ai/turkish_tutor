"Değil mi?" - "isn't it?", "right?", "aren't they?" It is used as an equivalent to molds such as known as "tag question" in English, these structures add an additional question to a sentence for confirmation or confirmation. "Değil mi?" is added to the end of the sentence, waiting for an answer or confirmation from the other person.


Bu film harika, değil mi?
This movie is great, isn't it?
* Evet, bu film harika! 


Ev ödevini yaptın, değil mi?
You did the homework, right?
* Hayır, ödevini yapmadım. 


O çok zeki bir insan, değil mi?
He's a very smart person, isn't he?
* Evet, o çok zeki bir insan. 


Dün sinemaya gittin, değil mi?
You went to the cinema yesterday, didn't you?
* Hayır, gitmedim.
