ne? : what?
	nerede? : at where?
	neresi? : what is this place?
	

	bu : this
	burada : here
	burası : here
	for the nearest 
	şu : that
	şurada : over there
	şurası : over there
	slightly further away
	o : that
	orada : there
	orası : there
	for the most distant
	

DİKKAT ET!: “O” has 2 different meanings:


1. It can be “he, she, it” 
2. It can be “that”


Bu ne? - What is this?
* Bu, telefon. - This is a phone


Şu ne? - What is that?
* Şu, vazo. - That is a vase.


O ne? - What is that?
* O, bardak. - That is a cup.


Vazo nerede? - Where is the vase?
* Vazo ofiste. - Vase is in the office.


Telefon nerede? - Where is the phone?
* Telefon burada. - The phone is here.


Bardak nerede? - Where is the cup?
* Bardak mutfakta. - The cup is in the kitchen.

NOT: We always put a buffer “-n” before adding ANY SUFFIX to the “bu, şu, o”


Bunlar ne?  - What are these?
* Bunlar telefon. - These are phones.




Telefonlar nerede? - Where are the phones?
* Telefonlar burada. - The phones are here.


Şunlar ne? - What are these?
* Şunlar vazo. - These are vases.


Vazolar nerede? - Where are the vases?
* Vazolar burada. - The vases are here.


Onlar nerede? - Where are they?
* Onlar şurada. - They are over there.


Bardaklar nerede? - Where are the cups?
* Bardaklar orada. - The glasses are there.

Burası neresi? - What is this place? 
* Burası ev. - This is a house.


Şurası neresi? - What is that place?
* Şurası hastane. - That's the hospital.


Orası neresi? - What is that place?
* Orası okul. - That's school.


Burası neresi? - What is this place? 
* Burası istasyon. - This is the station.


Şurası neresi? - What is that place?
* Şurası market. - That’s the market


Orası neresi? - What is that place?
* Orası otel. - That's the hotel.

Bu kim? - Who is this?
* Bu, Ali. - This is Ali.




Şu kim? - Who is that?
* Şu, öğretmen. - That is a teacher.


O kim? - Who is that?
* O, müdür. - That is manager.


Bunlar kim? - Who are these?
* Bunlar Ali ve Ahmet. - These are Ali ve Ahmet.


Şunlar kim? - Who are those?
* Şunlar çocuk. - Those are kids.


Onlar kim? - Who are those?
* Onlar kadın. - Those are women.
