Today we will learn about possessive suffixes. First of all, you should know that these are not the same as "personal suffixes". In the second document, we learned the suffixes of the personal pronouns "I, you, he, we, you, they". Today, we will learn the suffixes of possessive pronouns "my, your, his, our, your, their". We will divide them into two.

	(benim) kalemim
	your pen

	our pen
	(bizim) kalemimiz
	your pen 
	(sizin) kaleminiz
	their pen
	onların kalemi or kalemleri
	

b. for vowel ending
my money
	(benim) param
	your money
	(senin) paran
	his/her money
	(onun) parası
	our money
	(bizim) paramız
	your money
	(sizin) paranız
	their money
	onların parası or paraları
	As we can see in the two tables above, we only take the separator "-s" with the third person in words ending with a vowel, and drop the vowel in the others.

When we use possessive suffixes with "var" and "yok":


Kalemin var mı? - Do you have a pen?
- Evet, kalemim var. - Yes, I have a pen.
- Hayır, kalemim yok. - No, I don’t have a pen. 


Araban var mı? - Do you have a car?
- Evet, arabam var. - Yes, I have a car.
- Hayır, arabam yok. - No, I don’t have a car.


Paran var mı? - Do you have money? 
- Evet, param var. - Yes, I have money.
- Hayır, param yok. - No, I don’t have money.
