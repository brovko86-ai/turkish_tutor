The most used tense in Turkish is "Present Continuous Tense”.  And is used to talk about general habits, everyday actions and present. It is one of the easiest tenses in Turkish. After you get used to the melody of this verb, the present tense verb will naturally come out of your mouth :)


POSITIVE 
 
In positive sentences, there are slight differences according to the end of the verb root. Let's take an overview of all this first. And then let's examine it one by one.


FOR “CONSONANT” ENDING: 
(verb root) + (-ı, -i, -u, -ü) + (yor) + (personal suffix)


If the verb root ends with a consonant, we add one of the suffixes
"-ı, -i, -u, -ü" before adding the suffix "-yor" to make it easier to pronounce.


FOR “I,İ,U or Ü” ENDING:
(verb root) + (yor) + (personal suffix) 


If the verb root ends with "-ı, -i, -u, -ü", no extra letter is needed.


FOR “E” ENDING: 
(verb root) + (to make last letter “i” or “ü”) + (yor) + (personal suffix)


If the last letter of the verb root ends in "-e", we replace the last letter with "-i" or "-ü" for better pronunciation.


FOR “A” ENDING:
(verb root) + (to make last letter “ı” or “u”) + (yor) + (personal suffix)


If the last letter of the verb root ends in "-a", we replace the last letter with "-ı" or "-u" for better pronunciation.

NEGATIVE 


(verb root) + (-m) + (-ı, -i, -u, -ü) + (yor) + (personal suffix)


To make the verb negative, the letter "-m" comes right after the verb root. To make it easier to pronounce, "-m" follows "-ı, -i, -u, -ü".


NOTE: Unlike positive sentences, one rule is followed for ALL VERBS.

QUESTION


To turn the verb into a question sentence, as we learned earlier, we add the question suffix "mı, mi, mu, mü" after the suffix "-yor" and complete the verb by adding the person suffix at the end of the verb.


NOTE: Since we will make a positive sentence again, we follow the rule according to the last letter of the verb roots.


FOR “CONSONANT” ENDING: 
(verb root) + (-ı, -i, -u, -ü) + (yor) + (mı mi mu mü) + (personal suffix)


FOR “I,İ,U or Ü” ENDING:
(verb root) + (yor) + (mı mi mu mü) + (personal suffix) 


FOR “E” ENDING: 
(verb root) + (to make last letter “i” or “ü”) + (yor) + (mı mi mu mü) + (personal suffix)


FOR “A” ENDING:
(verb root) + (to make last letter “ı” or “u”) + (yor) + (mı mi mu mü) + (personal suffix)


(verb root) + (FOLLOW ONE OF THE ENDING RULES + (yor) + (question suffix) + (personal suffix)
