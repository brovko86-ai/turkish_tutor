"İsim Tamlaması - Compound Noun" is the combination of two or more word groups and their use instead of a single noun. 


a. Definite Compound Noun - Belirtili İsim Tamlaması 
b. Indefinite Compound Noun - Belirtisiz İsim Tamlaması 
c. Compound Noun with no suffixes - Takısız İsim Tamlaması 


c. Compound Noun with no suffixes - Takısız İsim Tamlaması 


It’s used when the first noun indicates the material of the second noun:


FIRST NOUN: - (material)
SECOND NOUN: - (following noun)


ipek eşarp - silk hijab 
yün çorap - wool socks
tahta masa - wooden table
altın madalya - golden medal
çelik kasa - steel safe
demir çivi - iron nail


what the second one looks like or resembles: 


FIRST NOUN: - (resemble)
SECOND NOUN: - (following noun)


kiraz dudak - cherry lip
elma yanak - apple cheek
canavar adam - monster man
yumurta kafa - egghead


the first element can be an ordinal number:


FIRST NOUN: - (number)
SECOND NOUN: - (following noun)


üç litre - 3 litres
beş kuruş - 5 cents 
beş adım - 5 steps
yedi kat - seven floor
