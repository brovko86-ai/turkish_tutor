"İsim Tamlaması - Compound Noun" is the combination of two or more word groups and their use instead of a single noun. And it is used to describe a particular object or group of people that functions as a whole.
And there are two different compound noun. These are:


a. Definite Compound Noun - Belirtili İsim Tamlaması 
b. Indefinite Compound Noun - Belirtisiz İsim Tamlaması 


But in this document we will only consider the first. 


a. Definite Compound Noun - Belirtili İsim Tamlaması 


When two nouns come together with a "belonging" relationship, we follow the definite compound noun. And order is very important when creating compound nouns. Let’s illustrate the importance of this order with a few examples:


(anne:mom)(tişört:t-shirt) 
annemin tişörtü = my mom’s t-shirt 


"the word that refers to the whole noun" gets:
FIRST NOUN: -(n)ın, -(n)in, -(n)un, -(n)ün


“the word pointing to the part attached to the name” gets:
SECOND NOUN: -(s)i, -(s)ı, -(s)u -(s)ü


NOTE: If the word ends with a vowel, it takes the "-n" and “-s” separator.
If it ends with a consonant it doesn’t take separator.
