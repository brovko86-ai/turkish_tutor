# Lesson 28 — -DIR, -DİR, -DUR, -DÜR — Certainty suffix

The suffix “-dır, -dir, -dur, -dür” adds meaning certainty. And we use it in the third person.And this suffix is not mandatory. When it is not added to the sentence, the sentence will be grammatically correct. And it's also often used in statement sentences. 


“Ayşe evdedir.” or “Ayşe evde.”
- Ayşe is at home.


“Bu elma tatlıdır.” or “Bu elma tatlı.”
- This apple is sweet.


“Köpek bahçededir.” or “Köpek bahçede.” 
- The dog is in the garden.


“O meşhur bir doktordur.” - “O meşhur bir doktor.” 
- She is a famous doctor.
