# Lesson 27 — -ARAK, -EREK — "by doing / while doing"

_Source: Google Doc `1_6oYVBAmbg0TexCWJLq6Bugs6Q__PmHa3oB64l8iYws` (Cowork project курс), pulled via export?format=txt._

---

The suffixes "-arak" and "-erek" are used in Turkish to express how an action is done or performed together with another action. These suffixes are added to the end of the verb as "-arak, -erek", establishing a relationship between two connected actions.  Let's understand better with example sentences:


ÖRNEKLER: 


Sinem kitap okuyarak müzik dinliyor. 
Sinem listens to music while reading a book.


Elif kahve içerek Netflix izliyor.
Elif watches Netflix while drinking coffee.


Ali televizyon izleyerek dinleniyor. 
Ali is resting while watching TV.


Hasan yüzerek spor yapıyor. 
Hasan does sports by swimming.
