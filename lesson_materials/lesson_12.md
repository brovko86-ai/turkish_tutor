Before we start learning this suffix, let's take a look at the case suffixes in general. There are five case suffixes in Turkish. These are:


CASES →
	LOCATIVE
	DATIVE
	ABLATIVE
	INSTRUMENTAL
	ACCUSATIVE
	TÜRKÇE →
	-de, -da
	-e, -a
	-den, -dan
	-le, -la
	-ı, -i, -u, -ü
	MEANING →
	in, on,at 
	to (direction)
	from
	with
	like “the”
	

These are the most important suffixes in Turkish. Because every verb in Turkish has a suffix followed by it. But don't worry, you don't need to memorize it as most of it makes sense. You will only learn very few of these verbs by hearing or practicing. (If you are wondering about this subject now, you can take a look at the "LIST OF VERBS" document I shared with you)


We discussed the suffix "-de, -da" , "-e, -a" and “-den -dan” in our previous documents and made exercises. Today, we will continue with the suffix “-le, -la” it shows the state of "instrumental". 


Now let's practice some of the most used verbs in daily life:


(-le, -la buluşmak:to meet with)
Kiminle toplantı yapıyorsunuz? - Who are you meeting with?
Onlarla akşam toplantı yapıyoruz. - We have a meeting with them in the evening.


Kiminle kafede buluşuyor? - Who is he meeting at the cafe?
Onunla kafede buluşuyor. - He meets her at the cafe.


Yarın akşam kiminle restoranda buluşuyorsun? - Who are you meeting at the restaurant tomorrow night?
Yarın akşam seninle restoranda buluşuyorum. - I'm meeting you at the restaurant tomorrow night.




(-le, -la evlenmek:to marry with)
Bu kız kiminle evleniyor? - Who is this girl marrying?
Bu kız Türk bir erkekle evleniyor. - This girl is getting marrying to a Turkish man.


Ne zaman evleniyorsun? - When are you getting married?
Gelecek ay evleniyorum! - I'm getting married next month!


Kardeşimle aynı gün evleniyoruz. - My sibling and I are getting married on the same day.




(-le, -la ilgilenmek:to take care of/to be interested in)
Annem neyle ilgileniyor? - What is my mother interested in?
Annem çiçeklerle ilgileniyor. - My mother takes care of the plants.


Veteriner neyle ilgileniyor? - What does the veterinarian care about?
Veteriner kedilerle ilgileniyor. - The veterinarian takes care of the cats.


Doktor kiminle ilgileniyor? - Who is the doctor taking care of?
Doktor, hasta kardeşimle ilgileniyor. - Doctor takes care of my sick sibling.




(-le, -la konuşmak:to talk with)
Derste kiminle konuşuyorlar? - Who are they talking to in class?
Derste öğretmenle konuşuyorlar. - They are talking to the teacher in the lesson.
 
Bu akşam eşimle telefonda konuşuyoruz. - Talking to my wife on the phone tonight.


Yarın akşam patronla ofiste konuşacaksın. - You're going to talk to the boss at the office tomorrow night.




(-le, -la oynamak:to play with)
Neyle oynuyor? - What is he playing with?
Tavşanla oynuyor. - He is playing with the rabbit.


Çocuklar neyle oynuyor? - What are the children playing with?
Çocuklar topla oynuyor. - The children are playing with the ball.


Karla oynamak istiyoruz. - We want to play with the snow.
