"İsim Tamlaması - Compound Noun" is the combination of two or more word groups and their use instead of a single noun. And it is used to describe a particular object or group of people that functions as a whole.
And there are two different noun phrases. These are:


a. Definite Compound Noun - Belirtili İsim Tamlaması 
b. Indefinite Compound Noun - Belirtisiz İsim Tamlaması 


In this document, we will continue with the second one.


b. Indefinite Compound Noun - Belirtisiz İsim Tamlaması 


The first word is the attribute noun and the second word is the noun. The relationship between the two names is "What type of?/What kind of?" we can say. It is used quite often in the Turkish language.


FIRST NOUN: -
SECOND NOUN: -(s)i, -(s)ı, -(s)u -(s)ü - 


(kiraz:cherry)(ağaç:tree)
kiraz ağacı - cherry tree [what type of tree?]


(okul:school)(çanta:bag) [what kind of bag?]
okul çantası - the school bag


(yemek:food)(bıçak:knife) [what type of knife?]
yemek bıçağı - food knife


(kiraz:cherry  reçel:jam) [what kind of jam?]
kiraz reçeli - cherry jam 


(zeytin:olive  yağ:oil) [what type of oil?]
zeytin yağı -  olive oil   


(kaza:accident  victim:kurban) [what kind of victim?]
kaza kurbanı - accident victim  


(yaz:summer   yağmur:rain) [what kind of rain?]
yaz yağmuru - summer rain




NOTE: Indefinite compound nouns are very suitable for joining two nouns together.


kireç (lime) + taş (stone) = kireçtaşı (limestone)
 
buz (ice) + dolap (cupboard)  =  buzdolabı (refrigerator)


dil (language) + bilgi (knowledge)  =  dilbilgisi (linguistics)
 
soy (ancestor) + ad (name)  =  soyadı (last name)
 
ayak (foot) + kap (container)  =  ayakkabı (shoes)


kahve (coffee) + renk (color) = kahverengi (brown)
